Anchored selectors

Every target is an element that describes itself badly — no id, no test hook, no accessible name, no text. What differs is what sits around it. Driven by scripts/e2e-anchor.mts.

A — durable ancestor, one child

B — two identical children under the target

C — sibling before the target

D — sibling before, and two identical children

E — repeated rows under one anchor

F — nothing durable in the neighbourhood

G — named by what it contains

H — stable id on the element

I — generated id on the element
